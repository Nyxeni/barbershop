About Us

A barbershop in the old tradition, built for today.

Maison Noir began with a single chair and a simple belief: a great haircut is a craft, not a transaction. A decade later, we still book generous appointments, sharpen our own razors, and know our clients by name.

Our space is quiet, considered and unhurried — a place to step away from the city for an hour and leave looking, and feeling, your best.
